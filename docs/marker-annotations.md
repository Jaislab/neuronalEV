# Biological marker annotations

This layer organizes the 37 capture markers for descriptive review. It does not change bead identity, gates, blank subtraction, detection rules or normalization. Primary display groups are editorial choices; overlapping association tags preserve other tissue/cell contexts. These are candidate associations, not validated cellular origins of captured material.

The input remains sample FCS files, an assay-blank FCS and the setup-bead FCS. No additional lineage-marker file is required. Actual data and gate assignments still require validation.

The primary groups list every capture marker once for display. This does not make the underlying associations exclusive: CD90 also has neuronal associations; CD13, CD31 and CD36 span vascular, immune and/or platelet contexts; CD63 and CD68 also have endolysosomal associations. The per-marker table and semicolon-separated tags retain those overlaps.

## Important interpretation limits

- CD171/L1CAM capture in plasma requires particular care because soluble L1CAM can occur. A positive bead signal alone does not establish neuronal EV origin.
- CD56/NCAM also occurs on NK cells. PSA-NCAM is a polysialylated epitope, not simply total NCAM expression.
- GLAST is astroglial-associated; O4 is oligodendroglial-associated; A2B5, CD140a/PDGFRA and CSPG4/NG2 have progenitor associations. Their sources overlap: PDGFRA includes mesenchymal contexts and CSPG4 includes pericytes and other tissues.
- CD11b, CX3CR1 and CD68 are relevant to microglial biology but also occur in peripheral myeloid populations. Their plasma signals do not distinguish microglial from peripheral EVs.
- O4, A2B5 and Ganglioside GD2 are glyco/lipid epitopes; they are not single gene products. CD133/1 and PSA-NCAM also involve epitope-level interpretation.
- VGLUT2 is SLC17A6 (UniProt Q9P2U8). Its vesicular localization and epitope accessibility matter for a surface-capture assay. VGLUT1/SLC17A7 is a different protein.
- CD9/CD63/CD81 are broadly EV-associated tetraspanins, not universal EV markers or evidence of one tissue source. The shared APC cocktail detects one or more accessible tetraspanins; it does not establish triple positivity on individual EVs.
- Protein expression references and tissue/cell biology do not validate recovery, purity, epitope accessibility or cellular origin in this ExoQuick plasma assay.

## What the implementation produces

- Versioned `marker_annotations.csv` and `marker_annotation_sources.json` snapshots in each new run; version and snapshot hashes in provenance.
- Six annotation columns appended to measurements, preserving values, row order and missingness: `biological_group`, `association_tags`, `gene_or_epitope`, `interpretation_note`, `annotation_source_ids`, `annotation_version`.
- Separate neuron-associated, glia/progenitor-associated and shared neural/progenitor heatmaps, plus the remaining marker views. All columns remain individual capture markers; full 37-marker profiles and two assay controls remain available.
- `marker_views/<group>_background_corrected.csv` and `<group>_normalized.csv` preserve individual per-acquisition marker values. Normalized views remain unavailable when the existing normalization rules cannot be satisfied.
- No summed APC score, neuronal/glial EV percentage, lineage classification, new significance test or new positivity threshold. Capture efficiencies differ across antibodies, so adding marker signals does not produce an EV count.
- Legacy runs without annotation snapshots can still render; a new analysis run is needed to create the versioned annotation outputs. Saved snapshots are used when reports are rerendered, rather than silently applying a newer installed annotation table.

## Sources and curation scope

Annotation version: **1.0.0**. Biological groups and tags are curated interpretive display annotations. They are not manufacturer-validated cell assignments, quantitative scores, or estimated cell-origin fractions. Manufacturer Table 1 establishes assay identities only.

The kit manual supplies the bead-to-marker map. Protein/gene entries and literature support interpretation; the grouping itself is a curated, provisional display layer, not a manufacturer-validated cell-of-origin panel.
